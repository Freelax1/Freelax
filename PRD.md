## 1. Executive Summary

Freelax is a UK-focused finance and tax management SaaS for sole traders, freelancers, and contractors. It unifies the day-to-day operations of running a one-person business — invoicing clients, tracking expenses, managing projects, calculating tax — into a single platform that is purpose-built around UK tax law (income tax, National Insurance, VAT, dividends, IR35).

The product replaces a typical freelancer's stack of three or four disconnected tools (a spreadsheet for income, a separate invoicing app, a receipts folder, an end-of-year accountant scramble) with one continuously-updated source of truth that is always ready for Self Assessment.

The strategic bet is that the UK's transition to **Making Tax Digital (MTD)** between 2026 and 2028 will force every freelancer to adopt HMRC-recognised software. Freelax is positioning to be the freelancer-native option in that market — simpler than Xero/QuickBooks, more compliant than spreadsheets, with AI-assisted bookkeeping baked in.

---

## 2. Problem

UK freelancers and contractors face a recurring set of pain points that no existing tool solves end-to-end:

1. **Tax is opaque.** A freelancer earning £60k doesn't know — in real time — what they owe, what they can deduct, or what they're allowed to spend. They find out in January, when it's too late to plan.
2. **Tools are fragmented.** Invoicing happens in one app, expenses in another, mileage in a third, IR35 status nowhere. Year-end is a manual reconciliation exercise.
3. **Generic accounting software is overbuilt.** Xero and QuickBooks are designed for businesses with employees, payroll, and accountants on retainer. A solo contractor doesn't need a chart of accounts; they need to know if a £400 laptop is deductible.
4. **MTD is coming.** From April 2026, sole traders earning over £50k are legally required to submit quarterly updates to HMRC via approved software. Spreadsheet-based bookkeeping becomes non-compliant overnight.
5. **IR35 is unmanaged.** Contractors carry real legal and tax risk per engagement and most have no structured way to assess or document status.

## 3. Target Users

**Primary**
- **UK sole traders and freelancers** earning £20k–£150k/year. Designers, developers, writers, consultants, tradespeople.
- **Limited company contractors** (single director) operating inside or outside IR35.

**Secondary**
- **Accountants** representing the above, who need clean exports and read access to client data.

**Not the target**
- Multi-employee businesses (use Xero/QuickBooks).
- Non-UK users (tax logic is UK-specific and not abstracted).

## 5. Scope

### 5.1 In scope (current)

**Income & invoicing**
- Create, send, and track invoices (PDF + public payment portal at `/inv/[token]`).
- Recurring invoices generated by daily cron.
- Quotes that auto-expire and convert to invoices on acceptance.
- Payment tracking via Stripe; chase emails for overdue invoices.
- Invoice templates with custom branding and bank details.

**Expenses & receipts**
- Manual expense entry with category and project tagging.
- AI-powered receipt scanning (Claude vision) — extracts merchant, amount, date, category from a photo.
- Mileage entries with HMRC-approved per-mile rates.
- Receipt attachments stored in Supabase storage.

**Clients & projects**
- Client CRM (contact, billing details, history).
- Project records with budget, status, and IR35 flag.
- Per-project P&L view.

**Tax**
- Real-time UK tax calculation: income tax (all bands), Class 2/4 NI, VAT (standard, flat-rate, cash accounting), dividend tax, personal allowance taper.
- "Safe to spend" / income forecast view.
- Tax pot — a virtual savings tracker for tax owed.
- Self Assessment export — Excel workbook + ZIP of receipts ready for the user or their accountant.
- IR35 assessment questionnaire with weighted scoring and AI-generated explanations.

**AI features (Claude)**
- Receipt OCR and auto-categorisation.
- Tax Q&A chatbot scoped to the user's own data.
- IR35 question explainers.
- Monthly financial narrative for Self Assessment.

**Accounts**
- Email/password auth (Supabase Auth), password reset.
- Plan tiers and limits enforced via [lib/plan-limits.ts](lib/plan-limits.ts).
- Stripe-hosted subscription checkout and customer portal.
- Accountant invite flow — read access to a user's data.
- Onboarding checklist.

**Platform**
- Progressive Web App with offline shell.
- Row-level security on every table, scoped by `user_id`.

### 5.2 In scope (next — MTD compliance)

Tracked separately in [MTD_COMPLIANCE_PRD.md](MTD_COMPLIANCE_PRD.md). High-level:
- HMRC OAuth (sandbox + production).
- Quarterly ITSA submissions for sole traders.
- VAT return submission (replaces current VAT export).
- Submission status tracking per period.
- Multi-business support for users with more than one trade.
- HMRC fraud-prevention headers on every submission.

### 5.3 Out of scope
- Payroll, pensions auto-enrolment, CIS.
- Multi-currency accounting beyond invoice-level FX.
- Double-entry bookkeeping / chart of accounts.
- Non-UK tax jurisdictions.
- Native mobile apps (PWA only).
- Bank feed integrations in v1 (manual import + receipt scan first; Open Banking is a fast-follow).

## 6. User Journeys

### 6.1 New freelancer onboarding
1. Signs up with email; lands on `/onboarding`.
2. Enters business name, trade type, VAT status, accounting period.
3. Adds first client, raises first invoice within 5 minutes.
4. Onboarding checklist nudges them to add bank details, upload a receipt, set tax year.

### 6.2 Day-to-day bookkeeping
1. Snap a receipt → Claude extracts the fields → user confirms in two taps.
2. Mark an invoice paid (or Stripe webhook does it automatically).
3. Dashboard updates revenue, profit, and tax-owed forecast in real time.

### 6.3 Self Assessment season
1. User opens `/tax` in early April.
2. Reviews summary; AI generates a one-page narrative explaining the year.
3. Exports the SA pack (Excel + receipts ZIP) and sends to accountant — or files themselves.
4. From FY 2026/27, the same screen submits quarterly updates direct to HMRC.

### 6.4 IR35 check
1. Contractor lands a new engagement; opens `/ir35`.
2. Answers ~15 weighted questions; each has an AI explainer.
3. Gets an inside/outside/borderline outcome with reasoning.
4. Outcome is stamped on the related project record.

## 7. Functional Requirements

| Area | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Auth | Email/password via Supabase; sessions enforced in [middleware.ts](middleware.ts); all `/api/*` and dashboard routes gated. |
| Tax engine | Pure functions in [lib/logic/tax-calculations.ts](lib/logic/tax-calculations.ts); 100% unit-tested; no DB calls. Tax rates are dated so historical years remain correct. |
| Invoicing | PDF rendered server-side via puppeteer-core + @sparticuz/chromium. Public token URLs unguessable (UUID v4). |
| Payments | Stripe Checkout for invoice payment; webhook updates invoice status atomically. |
| AI | Anthropic SDK; user data passed as context with per-user rate limits; no cross-user leakage. |
| Storage | Supabase storage buckets for receipts and logos; signed URLs only. |
| Email | Resend for invoice send, chase, quote send, accountant invite, password reset. Plain-text + HTML. |
| Cron | Vercel cron (daily 06:00 UTC) for recurring invoices and expiring quotes. |
| Export | Self Assessment pack: ExcelJS workbook + JSZip with original receipts; deterministic filenames. |
| RLS | Every table has a `user_id` column and a policy `auth.uid() = user_id`. No service-role calls from client code. |
| Plan limits | Enforced server-side in API handlers via [lib/plan-limits.ts](lib/plan-limits.ts). |

## 8. Non-Functional Requirements

- **Performance.** Dashboard TTI < 2.5s on 4G. Invoice PDF generation < 4s p95.
- **Availability.** 99.9% monthly. No scheduled downtime during UK working hours.
- **Security.** RLS on every table; no shared secrets in client bundles; Stripe + HMRC webhooks signature-verified; rate limits on all public endpoints (invoice token, quote token, webhooks).
- **Compliance.** UK GDPR; data residency in EU. SAR (Subject Access Request) export = SA export + account JSON. Hard delete within 30 days of cancellation.
- **Accessibility.** WCAG 2.1 AA on dashboard and invoice portal.
- **Offline.** PWA shell loads offline; queued reads from IndexedDB; writes require network in v1.
- **Testing.** Tax logic gated by Jest in the build (`build` script runs `jest && next build`).

## 9. Architecture

- **Frontend & backend:** Next.js 14 App Router on Vercel. Server Components for data fetching, Server Actions/Route Handlers for mutations.
- **Database:** Supabase Postgres with RLS. Schema in [supabase-schema.sql](supabase-schema.sql).
- **Auth:** Supabase Auth via `@supabase/ssr` cookies — no JWT round-trip in middleware.
- **AI:** Anthropic SDK called server-side only.
- **Payments:** Stripe (subscriptions + invoice payments).
- **Email:** Resend.
- **PDF:** puppeteer-core + @sparticuz/chromium on Vercel functions.
- **Observability:** Vercel logs + Supabase logs in v1; structured logging to a sink (Logtail/Axiom) before MTD launch.

## 11. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| HMRC recognition not granted before April 2026 | Existential — paid users churn to compliant competitors | Begin sandbox integration immediately; submit for recognition by Q4 2025; treat MTD as P0. |
| Tax calculation bug in production | Trust collapse, potential liability | Pure functions, dated rate tables, Jest in build pipeline, accountant beta panel reviewing edge cases. |
| AI hallucination in tax Q&A | User makes a wrong decision and blames Freelax | Disclaimers on every AI surface; ground answers in the user's own data; never give advice that isn't traceable to a source. |
| Stripe / Resend / Supabase outage | Invoices not sent, payments not captured | Idempotent webhooks, queue retries, status page, manual replay tools. |
| Scope creep into multi-user accounting | Dilutes positioning, slows MTD | Hard "no" on payroll, multi-currency, double-entry until 10k paid users. |
| Receipt OCR misclassifies expense category | Wrong tax outcome | Always show extracted fields for confirmation; never auto-submit without user review. |
