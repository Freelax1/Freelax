FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Is Freelax a registered tax adviser? +
Freelax provides real-time estimates based on current HMRC rules and the information you provide. We are not a regulated financial adviser. For complex tax situations, we recommend speaking with an accountant — Freelax makes that conversation much more productive because your records are already clean and accurate.
How accurate are the tax calculations? +
We maintain 2026/27 UK tax rates including Income Tax bands, Class 2 and Class 4 NI, dividend tax rates, student loan thresholds and the personal allowance taper. The calculations are updated every April when HMRC publishes new rates. Users who have verified against their accountants report figures within £50-200 of the final bill.
Does Freelax work for limited companies? +
Yes. Freelax handles sole traders, contractors and limited company directors. For Ltd directors, you can model salary + dividends combinations to see the most tax-efficient structure. IR35 assessments are specifically designed for Ltd company contractors.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes. No contracts, no minimum terms. Cancel from Settings at any time and you keep access until the end of your billing period. Your data is available to export as CSV or Excel before you leave.
